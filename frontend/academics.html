Academics

Semester structure, grading, and academic policies.

Semester Structure

The academic year runs across two semesters: Odd (August–December) and Even (January–May).

Grading & Attendance

A 10-point CGPA grading system is used. A minimum of 75% attendance is required to sit for semester-end exams.
