Admissions

Everything you need to know to apply to Greenfield College.

Eligibility

Candidates must have completed higher secondary education (10+2) with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in the relevant stream.

How to Apply

Fill out the online application, upload required documents, pay the application fee, and appear for the entrance test or submit qualifying exam scores.
